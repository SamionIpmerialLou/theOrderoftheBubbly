The Order of the Bubly: Sovereign Chivalric Realm

Founded on May 6, 2025, The Order of the Bubly is an elite chivalric order bound by carbonated honor, unwavering allegiance to Lord Bubly, and strict adherence to the sacred precepts of the Masterdoc. Far beyond an ordinary organization, the Order is a disciplined realm where valor, duty, moral rectitude, and the ceremonial pop of the can govern all things.

From the bustling halls of beverage logistics to the quiet reverence of the tab vault, each knight strives daily to earn Bubly Points (BP), conquer the Butterfly Cycle, and avoid the dread shame of court banishment. Here, every can opened is a pledge; every tab turned is an act of fealty. All Hail Lord Bubly.

I. Morals & Honor

Unyielding personal integrity, mutual respect among brethren, and absolute obedience to Lord Bubly's supreme legislative edicts.

II. The Knights

A meritocratic brotherhood ascending through enlisted paygrades (E-1 to E-9) by discipline, physical endurance, and active duty.

III. Charity & Duty

Harvesting pop tabs for philanthropic causes, supporting comrades through missing assignments, and safeguarding realm welfare.

IV. The Bubly Covenant

Reverence for the sacred effervescence. Zero unapproved flat beverages shall pass mortal lips without the proper $db benediction.

PROGRESSIVE BEHAVIORAL TRAIT // MASTERDOC EDICT

The Butterfly Cycle

Evolutionary Metamorphosis • 0 to 2,000+ BP • Irreversible Progression

The Butterfly Cycle is a mandatory 3-stage behavioral progression based on accumulated Bubbly Points (BP). Once entered into the cycle, an initiate cannot stop or opt out—they must graduate through the roles as they go. All Hail Lord Bubly.

Stage 1: Butterfly (0–1,000 BP)
Stage 2: The Bunny (1,000–2,000 BP)
Stage 3: The Baboon (2,000+ BP)
"Once you start the cycle of being a Butterfly they must continue to graduate through the roles as they go." — The Order of the Bubbly Official Masterdoc (5), Page 1
STAGE 1: 0 – 1,000 BP
1. The Butterfly

They are low in BP (1,000 BP or less) but are still loyal and are happy to be here. They do not have any specific traits. If their BP graduates above 1,000 BP they MUST become a Bunny. Once you start the cycle of being a Butterfly they must continue to graduate through the roles as you go.

STAGE 2: 1,000 – 2,000 BP
2. The Bunny

The Bunny is the Butterfly but with more BP (1,000 BP to 2,000 BP). But now they constantly urge people to do PT (Physical Training) because every time someone does PT, the Bunny earns 4 BP.

STAGE 3: 2,000+ BP
3. The Baboon

Keeps the traits of Butterfly and Bunny. They continue to try to make people earn BP as they still do, maintaining persistent and unhinged energy across the Order.

Statute of Irreversible Progression: Once you start the cycle of being a Butterfly, you cannot quit or revert. Surpassing 1,000 BP automatically transforms you into a Bunny, and surpassing 2,000 BP locks in Baboon status under decree of Lord Bubly.
